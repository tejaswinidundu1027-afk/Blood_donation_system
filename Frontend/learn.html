Everything you need to know about saving lives through blood donation.

Why Donate Blood?

Every 2 seconds someone needs blood. One donation can save up to 3 lives. Blood cannot be manufactured — it can only come from generous donors like you.

Who Can Donate?

Criteria Requirement
Age18 to 65 years

HemoglobinMinimum 12.5 g/dL
